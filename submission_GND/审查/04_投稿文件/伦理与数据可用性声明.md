# Ethics approval and consent to participate

This study analyzed exclusively publicly available, de-identified datasets (TCGA, CPTAC, CGGA, GEO, GLASS, DepMap, GDSC2, TCGASpliceSeq). No new human participants, tissue, or identifiable data were collected. CGGA data were used under the CGGA data-use agreement (registration required), and GLASS data were accessed under the GLASS consortium data-use terms via Synapse (syn17038081). As this work involves only secondary analysis of public de-identified data, it does not constitute human-subjects research; any journal-specific ethics documentation will be provided upon request.
