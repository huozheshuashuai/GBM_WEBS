# Highlights

- GBM recurrence resolves into two patient-resolved axes of proteomic evolution.
- Inter-patient recurrence heterogeneity exceeds technical variance by up to 260-fold.
- A frozen inflammatory–metabolic axis validates in an independent proteome cohort.
- GeoMx data support a mixed compositional–cellular model of the second axis.
- Near-zero average recurrence changes can mask bidirectional patient evolution.
