Subject: Presubmission inquiry — computational multi-omics study of post-transcriptional spliceosome accumulation in glioblastoma

Dear Editors,

I am writing to ask whether the British Journal of Cancer would consider a manuscript with the following profile for peer review.

Proposed title: "A writer-dominant expression state is associated with post-transcriptional spliceosome accumulation in glioblastoma"

Study type: Computational multi-omics analysis of public datasets (no new wet-lab experiments), with a fully reproducible open pipeline.

Core finding: In glioblastoma, an expression-defined writer-dominant state (Writer–Eraser Balance Score, WEBS) is associated with proteome-wide spliceosome accumulation (107/111 quantified proteins up-regulated in CPTAC-GBM) that tracks the state at the protein (ρ = 0.79) but not the mRNA level (ρ = −0.03), supporting a post-transcriptional regulatory pattern.

Supporting evidence: enriched spliceosome acetylation after parent-protein correction (OR = 2.29); abundance-dependent spliceosome phosphosite changes with elevated splicing-kinase abundance; 4,387 WEBS-associated alternative splicing events (TCGASpliceSeq) replicating in TCGA-LGG (83% sign-concordant); a six-gene prognostic signature externally validated in three independent glioma cohorts (GLASS HR = 2.14, p = 2.0 × 10⁻⁶; moderate discrimination, C-index 0.62–0.67); and computationally nominated candidate vulnerabilities (MED1, SF3B1, HDAC2, SIRT2) with CRISPR, drug-screen, and docking support.

Transparency features: negative and boundary results are reported explicitly (expression-matched permutation control, IDH-wild-type attenuation, moderate model discrimination); all code and the WEBS implementation are publicly available.

Given the journal's scope, I would appreciate your view on whether a purely computational study of this kind, with strong tumor-biology relevance but no new experimental validation, would be suitable for consideration. I would be glad to provide the abstract, figures, or a presubmission draft if helpful.

Thank you for your time.
